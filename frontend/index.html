What can police do if they arrest me without a warrant?
LG
Under Article 22 of the Constitution, you must be informed of the reason for arrest, allowed to consult a lawyer, and produced before a magistrate within 24 hours. Police cannot detain you beyond this without judicial approval. CrPC §57
